About This Project

This platform helps users organize and compare information recorded in different medical systems.

Important Disclaimer: This prototype is for medical terminology comparison and educational purposes only. The displayed mappings may be incomplete or unverified. They do not establish clinical equivalence and must not be used to diagnose conditions, select treatments, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Important: Text similarities do not establish clinical equivalence. This prototype does not diagnose diseases or recommend treatments.
